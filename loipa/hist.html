History Of Wræmberland

In 2022, Yiannis Lalos created a conlang named CGA' (Circular Graph A'), which was derived from Greek. Although this language was later lost, in 2023 another conlang, CGB' (Circular Graph B'), somewhat based on CGA', was created. CGB' is still used to this day in the Anthofōria Administrative Region.

On June 4, 2024, Michalis Lalos created a small garden in which he planted various plants and vegetables. On the same day, Ioannis Lalos II had the idea of making the garden a separate state. On June 5, 2024, Michalis Lalos agreed to this idea.

On June 6, 2024, both Michalis and Yiannis (II) Lalos declared the garden a separate state named Michalogiannía, ruled by a diarchy of Yiannis and Mihalis Lalos II. The diarchy was a very fast and efficient way of making the radical changes needed to make Michalogiannía a functioning state.

Shortly after, elections were held, which lasted for one (1) week. In the end, communism was voted for, and the nation changed its name and flag to Anthofōria.

During the same summer, two de facto states were established by Yiannis Lalos II: the Empire of the Black Rhombus (E.Bla.R.), which declared independence on 3 July 2024, and the Duchy of Nimborio (DoN), which declared independence on 7 August 2024.

In the fall of 2024, Yiannis Lalos believed that a language should be created in order to unify Anthofōrians; that is why Salamits was created.

In January 2025, Michalis Lalos, along with a friend of his, established a new government in the northern part of Anthofōria, thus creating great confusion in the country. As a result, on 2 February 2025, due to internal political conflicts, Anthofōria split into two countries: Storbuksia (also known as Stōrbuksland in Salamits) and Anthofōria. This event is remembered as The Great Division.

The Chairman of the newly founded Anthofōria was now Ioannis Lalos II.

As a result of the Great Division, there was a dispute concerning the borders of the two newly founded countries, which almost led to a conflict between the two. Thankfully, the borders were drawn peacefully.

Later, on 28 February 2025, Anthofōria’s leader initiated the creation of a union called the Salamic Union (Union of Salamic Nations, U.S.N.), uniting E.Bla.R., DoN, and the newly founded Anthofōria. The union adopted a new flag but maintained socialism as its ideology. This event is referred to as The Great Unification.

On 14 June 2025, the Liberated Ormós of Piraeus joined the U.S.N., just one day after declaring independence.

Due to inactivity, on 30 June 2025, known as Revolution Day, Yiannis Lalos, along with some citizens, went outside the borders of the U.S.N. and established a new country, the Republic of Cœperland, in the southern part of the U.S.N. During the night, they overthrew the U.S.N. government, placing Ioannis Lalos II as its leader.

Inspired by ancient Greek democracy but with socialist principles, they established a new government under the ideology of Socialist Neo-Participatory Democracy (SNPD).

The U.S.N. was renamed the Republic of Wræmberland, and on 2 August 2025, it was formally renamed the S.N.P.D. of Wræmberland.

On 11 August 2025, Wræmberland launched the “Ekfar Project”, which included:

  • Claiming a small island near the coast of Symi, now called the Island of Sun (Izlo Dẽ' Suna).
  • Constructing an artificial island named “To Nisi Tou Pedara”.
  • Between 25–26 August 2025, claiming the Big and Small Fruœpêr Islands and Big Iasonus de Mattarõs Island.
  • On 27 August 2025, the re-establishment of the government on the Liberated Òrmos of Piraeus.

After this, Wræmberland expanded into Sterea Ellada with the establishment of new territories.

On 27 August 2025, a de facto micronation was created under the name Southwest Union of Fragiadon (S-W. Union of Fragiadon). The following day, on 28 August 2025, this entity was formally integrated into the S.N.P.D. of Wræmberland and subsequently reorganized as an administrative region.

On 18 September 2025, Wræmberland became one of the two founding members of the Aegean Union, establishing it alongside the Republic of Bournias.
