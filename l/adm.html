Administrative Regions of Wræmberland
The Socialist Neo-Participatory Democracy of Wræmberland is divided into several constituencies, each with its own degree of autonomy but united under the principles of Socialist Neo-Participatory Democracy.

Each constituency elects or randomly selects its own representatives to attend the National Assemblies, while local governance is handled by citizen councils.

Constituencies may differ in their administrative roles — for example, districts are more populous and urbanized, while territories tend to be sparsely populated or historically significant.

This page provides an overview of the administrative regions of Wræmberland and their respective locations.
